The problem

Meta's business tools are fragmented: Business Suite, Creator Studio, WhatsApp Manager, Ads Manager, Instagram and Messenger inboxes. A founder running several brands lives in about 12 tabs.

The solution

One server

Brands are servers, Meta surfaces are channels, and your team and AI agents share the member list.

Git for agents

Every post, reply and ad draft is a commit with an author, a diff and a status.

Human approval gate

Jev binds approval to the hash of the exact payload. Changed payload, refused write. No auto-send.

MUSE voice harness

Push-to-talk assistant that reads and drafts, and can never approve on its own.

Who it's for

Businesses: one inbox for WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and Facebook leads, ads kept as drafts. Creators: one content studio and calendar across Facebook, Instagram and Threads.

Built with Llama. Meta product names belong to Meta. In this pitch nothing is connected to a live Meta account.
